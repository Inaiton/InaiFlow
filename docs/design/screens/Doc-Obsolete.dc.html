How obsolete works

ISO 9001 clause 7.5.3
Obsolete documents are kept for the audit trail but:
carry a red OBSOLETE watermark on every page
are hidden from operators and workflow steps
cannot be edited, deleted or restored to current
stay visible to auditors with read access
